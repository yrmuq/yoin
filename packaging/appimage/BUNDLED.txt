yoin is GPL-3.0-or-later. See NOTICE. The program is Rust and is linked
statically, including the tray (ksni) and the windows (slint, software
renderer). Qt, PySide, and CPython are not in this image.

Shared libraries copied next to the binary are listed in LIBS.txt. They
keep their own licenses. Typical names are fontconfig, freetype, wayland,
and xkbcommon. glibc is not bundled.
